⚡ 4-Pillar Core Architecture (RescueIQ PRD)

Decision-Support · Human in the Loop
📱

1. Citizen Intake

Mobile-optimized ground-level photo upload, automatic GPS capture, hazard categorization, and immediate tracking ID generation.

🤖

2. EfficientNet-B0 Vision

3-class damage classifier (Severe, Mild, Little-or-none) with an uncertainty abstention threshold (τ=0.50) to prevent forced false classifications.

🔗

3. Multimodal Deduplication

Combines Sentence-Transformers NLP embeddings, image dHash perceptual hash, GPS distance, and time proximity to group repeating reports.

⚖️

4. Transparent Priority Engine

Priority Score = 0.40·S + 0.25·U + 0.20·I + 0.15·A with factor breakdown, full coordinator overrides, and immutable audit logs.
